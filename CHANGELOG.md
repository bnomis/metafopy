# Changelog

## Unreleased (1.0 candidate)

- Audit 8, leftovers of the final run (see `docs/differences.md`): `regtest()` of a `trimfill()` fit refits with the labels 1 to k and `slab_null=False`, and the refit keeps the row names of the moderator matrix; `permutest()` of location-scale fits with `skip_beta`/`skip_alpha` returns the one-row `NaN` frames with R's column names, and `.coef()` of its result returns the two coefficient tables; `sandwiches` of `robust(club_sandwich=True)`; `rma_mv` `R` has an entry for every `~ 1 | factor` term; `dfbetas()` of `rma_mv` has syntactic column names (behaviour change); `blsplit(V, cluster, fun)` keeps the dimnames when `fun` returns a plain array; the row names of `Z_G1`/`Z_G2` follow the rows used. Harness: `c(1, NA, 3)` is a numeric vector in the R side of a case, `x$name <- NULL` of an absent field is a no-op, `to.long(append=)` indices are shifted to 0-based, and the known list matches the ids of `summary` cases.

- `metafopy.transf` uses short names: metafor's `transf.ilogit` is `metafopy.transf.ilogit` (and `transf.ipft.hm` is `metafopy.transf.ipft_hm`), so `from metafopy import transf` keeps the R spelling. The mechanical translations `transf_ilogit`, `transf_ipft_hm`, ... are now top-level functions (`metafopy.transf_ilogit`, the same objects). Behaviour change: `metafopy.transf.transf_ilogit` and the other `metafopy.transf.transf_*` names are gone.
- Eighth audit against metafor 5.2-1 (`bin/metafor-audit.sh`, second full run; 2277 causes before, 400 after; see `src/audit/README.md` for the holes the harness itself had and `docs/differences.md` for what stays different):
  - Arguments: `na_action` on the functions that read `getOption('na.action')`, `time=` on the fitting functions and methods, `rma(knha=, dfs=, i2def=, r2def=, skipr2=, abbrev=)`, `vif(joina=, joinb=, LB=)`, `anova(att=, Z=)` and the location-scale and selection `anova(L=, fixed=, test=, df=, refit=)`, `confint(parm=, extint=, digits=)`, `permutest(verbose=, fixed=)`, `profile(sub1=, startmethod=)`, `addpoly(cr_lb=, cr_ub=)`, `regplot(at_lab=, box_lty=)`, `rcalc(rowid=, vnames=, noid=)`, `pairmat(fixed=)`, `summary(signif_stars=)` of more result classes, control entries that other functions read are ignored like R does.
  - Fixed: bundled datasets that are `escalc` objects in R carry `measure`, `ni` and `digits`; `"NA"`, `"None"` and `""` strings in datasets are kept; `rma_mv` stores R's `0` placeholders for absent components and `level` as alpha; `simulate()`, `influence()` and `cumul()` follow `na.action`; `rma_ls` drops redundant scale columns and keeps the not-missing cells of `Z_f`; `profile()` and `confint()` position arguments of `rma_mv` and location-scale fits; `dfround` with `NA` digits is `NA`; `transf.pft`/`ipft` recycling; `regplot`/`plot_selmodel` scalar line settings; `rma_mv(restart=True)`; `bldiag` of a dict; `fsn(pool=)` case; `emmprep(transf=)` for more links; location-scale `permutest()` and `vif(sim=True)` refits are 50 to 200 times faster.
  - Results: R's bookkeeping fields (`ids`, `yi_f`, `vi_f`, `not_na`, `X_f`, `fit_stats`, `dfs`, `control`, ...) on all fit classes; `escalc`, `vcalc`, `ranef` (`rma_mv`) and `blsplit` printers; labelled matrices (`X`, `Z`, `G`, `H`, `hessian`, `vvc`) keep numpy compatibility; `logLik()` returns a float with `df`.
  - Behaviour changes: `fit_stats(REML)` is now `fit_stats_for(REML)` (`fit_stats` is R's table); `influence().inf` and `.dfbs` are data frames (the marks are `.marks`, the array `.dfbs_array`); `permutest().coef()` returns the coefficient table; `update(formula=)` is `formula_`; invalid `na_action` raises "Unknown 'na.action' specified under options()".
- Fifth audit against metafor 5.2-1 (metafor's own test suite and help examples translated; no R was available for this round, so the new tests check behaviour and the numbers quoted in the audit):
  - Inputs: `rma()` and `rma_mv` use the `slab` of `escalc` frames, `escalc(include=)` run twice keeps `ni`, row subsets and column subsets of `escalc` frames (`rbind_escalc`, `summary_escalc`) keep matching `ni`/`slab`; `rma_uni` and location-scale fits warn "k studies with NAs omitted from model fitting."; scalar `vi` and `weights` are recycled; a scale factor with `NA` drops those studies and `link='identity'` fits; `rma_mh` `RD`/`RR` without events no longer raise `ZeroDivisionError`; `emmprep(data=)`, `regtest` and `fsn` column resolution; `dfround` with `NA` digits; `labbe()` of `rma_uni` fits from counts; `factor()`, `relevel()`, an `NA`-skipping `scale()` and user functions in formulas.
  - `predict(newmods=)` matches names (partial too), `prob='>log(0.8)'` evaluates the cutoff, `addx=True` is printed; `anova()` of two models refuses ML against REML and fixed against random effects and warns for estimators other than ML/REML and non-nested models; `SelmodelResult.coef()` gives beta and delta, `PermutestResult.coef()`, `vcov()` of `deltamethod` and `matreg` results, `RMAResult.dfs`, the `g_*`/`h_*` fields of `rma_mv`; `anova(abbrev=)`, `influence(measure=)`, `profile(pred=, blup=, newmods=)`, `hettest(model=)`.
  - Printing: `digits=` and `signif_stars`/`signif_legend`; `progbar=` on `tes`, `baujat`, `permutest`, `cooks_distance`, `leave1out`, `cumul`, `rstudent`, `influence`, `dfbetas`; printed `anova(X=)` with Hypotheses and Results; `WaldResult.to_frame()` and `ConfintResult.to_frame()`; tables for `anova(btt=)` and `confint()` of multi-component `rma_mv` fits.
  - Plots: `forest()` labels for all back-transformations, `ilab_lab=` as a string, `addpoly(predict_result, predstyle='shade'|'dist')`, named colours in colour ramps, legend position names, abbreviated `predstyle`, `confint(xlim=)`, `forest(addcred=, clim=)`, `addpoly(addcred=)`, `plot_cumul(addgrid=)`, `layout` (deprecated), `plot_permutest(xlim=)`, `plot_vif(xlim=)`, `profile(cline=, pch=, ...)` with `log`/`cex`/`lwd` in `plot_profile`, `bty`, `las`, `main`, `xaxt` pass-through arguments.
  - Optimisers and control: `rma_mv(control=)` `optimizer`, `optmethod`, `nearpd` (the colditz1994 `cvvc` example gives `vvc`), `hesspack`, `hessianCtrl`, `mfmaxit`, `parallel`/`ncpus`/`cl`; further location-scale optimisers; `confint()` of location-scale fits accepts `vc.min`/`vc.max` and rejects unknown entries; `rma_glmm(control=)` `hessianCtrl`, `hesspack`, `dnchgcalc`, `dnchgprec`; tighter convergence of the derivative-free optimisers (`CM.EL` `tau2` of nielweise2007 now 0.693458, R 0.693459).
  - Datasets: aloe2013, baskerville2012, bonett2010, collins1985b, furukawa2003, hahn2001, kalaian1996, lehmann2018, nielweise2008 and viechtbauer2021 are bundled; hackshaw1998 and molloy2014 were checked against metadat 1.6-0.
- Optimiser agreement with R (`src/tests/r/generate_audit4_numerics.R`): `rma_mv(struct='GEN')` on a flat ridge of the likelihood (konstantopoulos2011) no longer fails with 'precision loss'; `permutest()` of location-scale fits with permuted scale fits on the boundary reproduces R's p-values and undefined standard errors; the identity-link scale standard errors are documented to the digit (see `docs/differences.md`).
